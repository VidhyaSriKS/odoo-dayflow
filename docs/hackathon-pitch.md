# Dayflow - Hackathon Pitch Deck & Value Proposition

## 1. Problem Statement
Modern enterprises face severe friction due to fragmented HR systems:
- Attendance is recorded in isolated punch clocks or manual spreadsheets.
- Leave requests get trapped in email threads or chat messages.
- Payroll visibility for employees is obscure, leading to support ticket overhead.
- HR admins lack real-time predictive insights to spot burnout, frequent tardiness, or department absenteeism trends.

## 2. The Solution: DAYFLOW
**Every workday, perfectly aligned.**

Dayflow unifies all HR verticals into a single cohesive SaaS application powered by real-time data analytics and an AI HR Assistant.

## 3. Key Innovations & Differentiators
1. **AI HR Assistant**: Queries live relational database metrics to instantly answer employee leave balances or HR management questions without manual filtering.
2. **AI Attendance Insights**: Detects tardiness patterns and burnout risks before they turn into employee turnover.
3. **Automated End-to-End Workflows**: Leave approval seamlessly updates leave balances, recalculates attendance records, dispatches notifications, and updates audit logs in one atomic transaction.
4. **Professional UI & PDF Generator**: Clean, modern dark/light dashboard with interactive Recharts visuals and downloadable salary slips.

## 4. Impact & Scalability
- Reduces HR operational overhead by **40%**.
- Increases employee transparency with instant payroll and attendance visibility.
- Enterprise-ready modular architecture with Spring Boot REST backend, React frontend, and Python FastAPI AI layer.
