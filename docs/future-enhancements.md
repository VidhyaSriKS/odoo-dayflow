# Dayflow Future Enhancements & Product Roadmap

The following features represent future extension vectors for Dayflow beyond the initial core release:

## 1. Biometric & Geolocation Attendance
- **Facial Recognition Check-in**: Integration with mobile camera or tablet station for biometric check-in.
- **Geofenced Check-in**: GPS boundary verification to allow field or remote staff to check in only from approved office locations or remote sites.

## 2. Advanced AI & Predictive Workforce Analytics
- **Burnout Risk Prediction**: Machine learning model analyzing hours worked, consecutive days, and leave patterns to flag potential employee burnout before turnover.
- **AI Sentiment Analysis**: Natural language processing on employee feedback surveys to measure department morale.

## 3. Recruitment & Onboarding Pipeline
- **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)**: Resume parsing, candidate interview scheduling, and offer letter generation.
- **Automated Employee Onboarding**: Interactive workflow guiding new hires through document submission, IT asset requests, and team introductions.

## 4. Multi-Tenant Enterprise & Mobile Apps
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Support for holding companies managing multiple independent subsidiaries.
- **Native iOS & Android Applications**: React Native mobile apps with push notifications for instant leave approvals and check-in widgets.
